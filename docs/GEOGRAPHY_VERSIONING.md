# Geography versioning

Schema separates jurisdictions, congressional districts, geography versions and district crosswalks. The 2026 candidate district vintage is Census 119th Congress (`CENSUS_BAS_2026_CD119`); this metadata does not mean geometry is loaded or legally verified. Keep full source geometry and boundary provenance separate from optimized browser geometry. County FIPS, precincts, congressional districts, and authorized crosswalks remain distinct. No county result can be painted as a district result without a supported crosswalk and source resolution.
